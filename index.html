Community guidelines

Three rules, so the signal never drops.

Orbiton stays useful because everyone holds the same three lines.

01
Keep it relevant
  • Discussions should stay on-topic.
  • Every message should add value to the community.
02
Build in public
  • Share your projects.
  • Share your learning journey.
  • Help other members grow.
03
No spam
  • No unnecessary forwards.
  • No unrelated promotions.
  • Keep discussions useful and high quality.

About Orbiton

Orbiton was founded by Ekansh Saxena as a builder-first student community — a place for students to explore AI, build products, prepare for hackathons, contribute to open source, and collaborate with like-minded students to create opportunities together.

It aims to be more than just a group chat. Orbiton is a place where students actively build, collaborate, and grow — trading passive learning for shipped work and real teammates.
